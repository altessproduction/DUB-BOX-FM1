What this is

The four DUB pages of the DUB-BOX firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1 — DUB (the DJ filter, the mix, the throw into the echo), SIREN, DELAY (the tape echo) and ISOLATOR (the four-band kill board) — running the same integer DSP as the firmware, ported line for line and validated bit-exact against the C. The audio sources feed the same chain the FM-1's USB and Bluetooth inputs do: isolator → filter → tape echo → out, the siren joined at the master.

Keyboard: Z X C V kill the ISO bands, space sounds the siren (hold).

The real thing

On the FM-1: OCT− enters the DUB screen and walks the pages, HOME leaves. K1–K3 (and ALGO) are the page's knobs, SEL is the page's switch, the mixer's mute keys kill the ISO bands, and REC (or SEL held) sounds the siren. PLAY mutes the master — the drop. USB audio in/out and the Bluetooth receiver (SBC/AAC/aptX) are the router's own, untouched. Install it on your FM-1 →
