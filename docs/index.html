A dub station firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1: the full USB audio interface and Bluetooth receiver of the audio router, plus the siren, the tape echo, the DJ filter and the four-band isolator on their own performance pages. No sequencer, no synths — the dub tools, over whatever you feed it.

The pages

DUBDJ filter (LP↔HP), mix, throw into the echo
SIREN5 LFO types × 5 waves, pitch 220–1200 Hz, hot
DELAYtape echo: time, feedback, level, tone
ISOfour bands 90/420/2600 Hz+, gains and kills

How it plays

OCT− enters the DUB screen from anywhere and walks the four pages; HOME leaves. K1–K3 (ALGO where a page has a fourth) are the page's knobs. SEL is the page's switch — the module on DUB, the siren's wave on SIREN. REC (or SEL held) sounds the siren at its level, from any screen. The mixer's mute keys kill the ISO bands, all four at once is the total kill. PLAY mutes the master — the drop. Everything persists: settings v17 carries the dub state across boots.

The station around it

Under the dub modules, the audio router runs whole: 2-in/2-out USB sound card with asynchronous feedback, Bluetooth receiver with SBC, AAC, aptX and aptX HD (own clean-room stack, no vendor blobs), mixer with trims and mute/solo, five-band EQ and peak limiter, meters, waveforms and spectrum. The dub chain sits between the mix and the master FX, and rests bit-transparent when off.
