Firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

DUB-BOX: the FM-1 as a dub station — USB audio interface and Bluetooth receiver, with the siren, the tape echo, the DJ filter and the four-band isolator on their own pages.

What you need

DUB-BOX is built on the fm1-audio-router platform, which runs on the m-bootloader (a community bootloader for the FM-1, by jvitkauskas). If your FM-1 still runs the stock M-VAVE firmware (or SLOOP), install the m-bootloader once with M-Flasher, then every DUB-BOX update is an ordinary firmware write.

Before any write: keep a verified full-flash backup and a working recovery route for your own unit — a settings export is not a firmware backup. The MvaveFM1Unbricker project provides backup/recovery tooling. Never erase the whole chip.

Install (M-Flasher)

  1. Download dub-box-1.0.0.fwsc — from this site or from the GitHub release.
  2. Check its SHA-256 against SHA256SUMS.
  3. Turn the FM-1 off, then on while holding REC (the m-bootloader's update mode).
  4. Open M-Flasher, select the FM-1 and the .fwsc. Keep “replace bootloader” disabled and “erase settings and Bluetooth pairings” unchecked.
  5. Flash, then verify: play USB audio, pair Bluetooth (hold OCT+), press OCT− for the DUB pages.

First sounds

Plug the FM-1 into your computer (it appears as a 2-in/2-out USB sound card) or pair it as a Bluetooth speaker. Press OCT−: the DUB screen. SEL switches the module on. Turn K1 for the DJ filter, K3 to throw the sound into the tape echo, walk to DELAY for the tape itself, to SIREN, hold REC — and PLAY is your drop switch.

Notes

DUB-BOX identity FM-1_235 — it installs beside SLOOP's own FM-1_234 scheme without colliding. Its settings carry over from the audio router's (v16 → v17); SLOOP's projects do not apply. Firmware-only updates preserve the device's m-bootloader, settings and Bluetooth bonds.
